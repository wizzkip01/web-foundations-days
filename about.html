About QuickNotes

QuickNotes helps you capture thoughts, reminders, and ideas as soon as they come to mind. Keep your notes in one simple place so you can focus on what matters.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your thought into the New note field.
  2. Select Add Note to save it to your list.
  3. Review your notes whenever you need a reminder.

Features

  • Quickly capture ideas and reminders.
  • See your notes together in one list.
  • Keep track of how many notes you have.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Tab Move to the next control.
Shift + Tab Move to the previous control.
Enter Submit the note form when the Add Note button is focused.
